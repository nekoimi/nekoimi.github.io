For a personal blog, theme choice is mostly about reducing friction:

- the homepage should help readers find recent writing quickly
- article pages should prioritize content over decoration
- navigation should stay obvious without becoming noisy

AstroPaper is a good reference for that balance. It keeps the structure familiar while leaving enough room to adapt the site to personal needs.
