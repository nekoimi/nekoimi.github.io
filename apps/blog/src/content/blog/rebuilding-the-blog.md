The previous blog implementation was serviceable, but the presentation felt too heavy for a personal writing space.

This rebuild focuses on a cleaner reading experience:

- a simpler homepage
- a clearer post index
- tag pages instead of query-string filtering
- a calmer typography-first post layout

The new structure is intentionally minimal so it can grow with future posts instead of being tied to product marketing blocks.
